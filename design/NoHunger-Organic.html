How it works

Three simple steps to zero waste.

Sign up as donor or receiver

Create your profile in under a minute. Switch roles anytime.

List your extra food live

Snap a photo, add portions and a pickup window. It goes straight to the map.

Neighbors claim & collect

A neighbor picks it up in person. No apps for delivery, no middlemen.

What's inside

Thoughtful features, built around real neighborhoods.

Live Map

See fresh listings pop up around you in real time — no refresh needed.

Time-aware freshness

Each listing tracks how long ago it was made, with visual freshness signals.

Portion control

Claim only what you need — the rest stays available for the next neighbor.

Zero waste auto-removal

Expired listings drop off automatically. Nothing lingers, nothing forgotten.
